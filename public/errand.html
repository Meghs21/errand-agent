How this works

Errand is an ordinary AgentPass client. It has no PayPal credentials. Every payment goes to AgentPass, which checks the mandate's limits, asks a second AI whether the purchase fits the mandate's purpose, then captures the payment, puts it on hold for the owner, or blocks it.
